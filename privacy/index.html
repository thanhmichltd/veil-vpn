Privacy Policy — Veil VPN

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Veil VPN ("the App") is developed by Thanh Mich LTD ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the Veil VPN Android application only.

If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the App.

1. Overview

Veil VPN is a VPN (Virtual Private Network) tool. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user account or profile anywhere in the App — you can connect and use every core feature without giving us your name, email, or any identity information.

Because Veil VPN's whole purpose is to route your internet traffic through a VPN server of your choosing, please read Section 2.1 carefully — it describes exactly what that means for your traffic and why we cannot make blanket "we never log anything" promises about servers we do not operate.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Your VPN connection and traffic — how routing actually works

When you tap Connect, the App uses Android's built-in VpnService to open a tunnel and route the traffic of the apps you select (or all apps, if you don't use split tunneling) through the VPN server you picked in the server list. Android will show you its own "Connection request" system dialog every time a VPN tunnel starts — this is device-level protection, not something the App controls.

What we do: the App itself does not run its own traffic-inspection or logging code, does not read the content of your traffic, and does not build a browsing-history profile on our servers.

What we're honest about: the free-tier server locations bundled with the App are third-party relay servers that Thanh Mich LTD does not own or operate. Because we don't control that infrastructure, we cannot verify or guarantee the logging practices of those relay operators — any connection-level logs they keep (such as connection timestamps, source/destination IP, or data volume, which relay operators may retain for their own network operation, security, or abuse-prevention purposes) are outside our control. If a "no-logs" claim matters to you, we'd rather tell you this plainly than make a promise about servers we don't run. Premium server configurations bundled for paying subscribers are separate configuration files from the same App but are likewise provided by third-party server operators, not hosted by Thanh Mich LTD.

We recommend avoiding the App for activity where you need an independently audited no-logs guarantee.

2.2 Check IP feature (ip-api.com)

The in-app "Check IP" screen sends your device's current public IP address to the third-party service ip-api.com to look up and display your IP address, approximate location, and internet service provider. This request is made directly from your device to ip-api.com; we do not store the result on any server we operate. See ip-api.com's own terms for how they handle this request.

2.3 Split tunneling ("App Using VPN")

To let you choose which installed apps route through the VPN, the App reads the list of apps installed on your device (Android QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission). This list is processed entirely on your device to build the toggle list you see — it is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

2.4 Usage and diagnostic data (Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand how the App is used and to diagnose crashes. This may include app interaction events, screens viewed, approximate device/app information, and crash logs/stack traces. We use Firebase Remote Config to adjust App behavior remotely; this is unrelated to your VPN traffic or connection history.

2.5 Advertising data (Google AdMob and mediation partners)

The App includes the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK and bundles the Adjust, Meta (Facebook) Audience Network, and TikTok Business SDKs for possible ad mediation. Ads are not yet configured in the current release build — the App's build process does not allow a release build to ship without setting this up first — but the SDKs and ad placements in the App are wired and may begin serving ads in a future update. When active, AdMob and its mediation partners may access your device's advertising identifier and related device/ad-interaction signals to serve and measure ads — including, on supported Android versions, through Android's Privacy Sandbox (Topics/Attribution) APIs — and, where applicable under your region's rules and consent choices, to personalize them. See Google's policies: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. None of the mediation SDKs (Adjust, Meta, TikTok) is configured with an account token in this build, so none of them currently collects or reports data; we will update this policy before any of them is enabled.

2.6 Purchase data (Google Play Billing)

Veil VPN offers an optional premium subscription (weekly with a 3-day free trial, or monthly) and a one-time lifetime purchase that unlock premium server locations and remove ads. All purchases are processed entirely through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles your payment method and provides us only your purchase/entitlement status (which plan is active) so the App can unlock premium features for you. We never see or store your card details.

2.7 Information we do not collect

Veil VPN does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contact list. The App requests no camera, microphone, or photo-library access, and does not access your precise device location (the "location" shown on the Check IP screen is derived by the third-party ip-api.com service from your public IP address, not from your device's GPS).

3. How we use information

We use the information above to: establish and operate your VPN connection, run the Check-IP and split tunneling features, diagnose and fix crashes, understand aggregate usage to improve the App, process your premium purchase, and — once configured — show and measure ads.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers and infrastructure operators that power the App's functionality:

  • Third-party VPN relay operators — your routed traffic passes through the server you select; see Section 2.1 for what we do and do not control about this.
  • ip-api.com — receives your public IP to power the Check-IP screen.
  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) — usage analytics and crash diagnostics.
  • Google AdMob and its mediation/ad-network partners — to serve and measure ads, once active.
  • Google Play — to process your subscription or lifetime purchase.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Thanh Mich LTD, our users, or others.

5. Data retention

Your selected server, language, appearance choice, split-tunneling selections, and purchase status are stored locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage. Analytics, crash, advertising, and purchase data are retained by Google according to its own retention policies. Connection-level logs, if any, kept by third-party VPN relay operators are retained according to their own policies, which we do not control (see Section 2.1).

6. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience privacy/VPN tool, not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the details in Section 8.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Advertising ID: reset or opt out of interest-based advertising via Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Split tunneling: choose exactly which apps route through the VPN, or turn split tunneling off, from the App's "App Using VPN" screen.
  • Local data: uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps, removes your locally stored preferences and purchase flag.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (e.g., under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA) to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or object to certain processing. Contact us at the email in Section 8 to exercise these rights.
