FAQ
Do I need an account to use Veil VPN?

No. Connecting, choosing a server, split tunneling, and Check IP all work from the first launch — there's no sign-up anywhere in the app.

Is Veil VPN really free?

Free servers are available in the US, Japan, and South Korea with ads. A Premium subscription (Weekly with a 3-day free trial, Monthly, or a one-time Lifetime purchase) removes ads and unlocks additional server nodes in those same countries — pricing is shown upfront before you subscribe, and Weekly/Monthly renew automatically until cancelled.

Do you keep logs of my browsing?

The app itself doesn't run traffic-inspection or logging code. Free-tier servers are third-party relay infrastructure we don't operate, so we can't make a verified "no-logs" guarantee about them — see the full Privacy Policy for the honest details.

What is split tunneling?

"App Using VPN" in Settings lets you pick exactly which installed apps route through the VPN tunnel and which use your normal connection — the installed-app list is read on-device only and never leaves your phone.

What languages does it support?

12 interface languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic, Russian, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, and Chinese.
